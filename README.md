# Dodge Bullets - Survivor Game

A fast-paced action RPG inspired by Vampire Survivors. Fight endless waves of enemies, unlock powerful skills, and survive as long as you can!

### TRY NOW: https://baolongdinh.github.io/dodge_bullets_game
## Features
- Auto-shooting projectile system
- 6 Active Skills (Q, W, E, R, T, Y)
- **6 Passive AOE Skills** with evolution mechanics
- Infinite scaling difficulty
- Item collection and stat upgrades
- Score tracking system

## How to Play

### Controls
- **WASD/Arrow Keys**: Movement
- **Q**: Fireball Barrage (AoE damage)
- **W**: Spirit Wolves (summon allies)
- **E**: Shield Slam (dash + stun)
- **R**: Black Hole (pull + damage)
- **ESC**: Pause
- **M**: Toggle music

###Skills

#### Active Skills
- **Fireball Barrage (Q)**: Launches multiple fireballs in all directions
- **Spirit Wolves (W)**: Summons wolves that attack nearby enemies
- **Shield Slam (E)**: Dash forward, stunning and damaging enemies
- **Black Hole (R)**: Creates a vortex that pulls and damages all enemies
- **Phoenix Rebirth (T)**: Passive - Revive once when killed
- **Critical Overload (Y)**: Passive - Every 20 crits triggers mega-crit

#### Passive AOE Skills (NEW!)

**⚡ Lightning Ring** (Unlock: Level 3+, Max: Level 9)
- Rotating electric rings deal continuous damage
- Level 5: +1 ring (counter-rotating)
- Level 9: Chain lightning to nearby enemies

**🌀 Blade Orbit** (Unlock: Level 5+, Max: Level 9)
- Orbiting swords slash enemies automatically
- Scales: 3 → 6 → 9 blades
- Metallic gradient visual with motion blur

**🔥 Fire Aura** (Unlock: Level 7+, Max: Level 9)
- Continuous fire damage + burn DOT
- Level 5: Burn spreads to nearby enemies
- Level 9: Enemies explode on death

**❄️ Ice Nova** (Unlock: Level 9+, Max: Level 9)
- Periodic frost wave (every 5s)
- Slows enemies by 50%
- Level 5: Freezes enemies for 0.5s
- Level 9: Leaves frozen ground

**☠️ Poison Cloud** (Unlock: Level 11+, Max: Level 9)
- Toxic fog deals stacking DOT
- Lingers 1s after leaving cloud
- Level 9: Poison stacks up to 3x

**🌟 Holy Water** (Unlock: Level 13+, Max: Level 9)
- Drops golden puddles periodically
- Damages enemies standing in them
- Level 5: Slows enemies by 30%
- Level 9: Chain explosions on expiry

## Difficulty Modes
- **Easy**: 0.6x multiplier, 150 HP, slower spawns
- **Normal**: 1.0x multiplier, 100 HP, balanced
- **Hard**: 1.5x multiplier, 75 HP, faster spawns
- **Nightmare**: 2.5x multiplier, 50 HP, extreme challenge
