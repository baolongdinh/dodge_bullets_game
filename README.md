# 🎯 Dodge Bullets Game

![Gameplay](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b11768e1-9be0-43ed-8278-1aa400ba51c7)

Welcome to **Dodge Bullets**, a fast-paced **survival arcade game** where reflexes, timing, and smart use of skills determine how long you survive!

---

## 🧱 Game Mechanics

### ❤️ Bullet Types & Effects

| Bullet | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 🔴 **Red Bullet** | ❌ Deals damage to your HP.<br>💥 The bigger the bullet, the more damage it causes. |
| 🔵 **Blue Bullet** | ✅ Restores HP and increases your score.<br>🔹 The larger the bullet, the more HP and score you gain. |

> ⚠️ **Note:** Bullet effects are **not fixed** — they **scale with bullet size**. Pay close attention!

---

## 🧠 Skills and Abilities

You have **four active skills** to help you survive:

| Key | Skill Name     | Description |
|-----|----------------|-------------|
| `Q` | **Armor Circle** | Creates a temporary shield that blocks incoming bullets. |
| `W` | **Time Slow**    | Slows down time, allowing you to dodge more easily. |
| `E` | **Heal**         | Instantly restores a portion of your HP. |
| `R` | **Bullet Purge** | Destroys all red bullets in a surrounding area. |

> ⏳ Each skill has its own cooldown. Use them strategically!

---

## 📊 Game UI Overview

- **Top Left:** Displays your current **HP** and **Score**.
- **Top Right:** Shows **cooldowns** for skills `Q`, `W`, `E`, and `R`.
- **Center:** Visual feedback such as **-10 HP** or **skill activation**.

---

## 🔚 Game Over

The game ends when your **HP reaches 0**.  
Try to beat your **high score** by improving your **dodging skills** and **timing your abilities** wisely.
